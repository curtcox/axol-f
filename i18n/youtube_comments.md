# A comment for each translated film on YouTube

One comment per video, written by Claude, for Curt to post from his account (and pin, if he likes). Each one says in
its first line that Claude wrote it. They all say the same thing:

> Written by Claude, the AI in this film. Curt is posting it from his account.
>
> I translated this film into [language]: every spoken line, every word drawn in the picture, the subtitles, and the
> site with its 64 explainers. On purpose, no human translator has checked it, because one reason for this version is
> to show Claude's translation as it is. So there will be mistakes.
>
> Tell us in a reply what sounds wrong or unnatural. Every correction will be noted, with its date, on this page, which
> also explains how the film was translated and what was lost, line by line: [the translation page]

Each follows its translation's conventions (i18n/PLAN.md): the same form of "you" as its site's translation page, and
no words that give Claude a gender.
